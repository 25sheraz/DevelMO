## 1. Company overview

DevelMo is a UK-registered AI, computer vision, cloud, and custom software company. It builds practical systems that put modern technology to work inside real businesses, scaling teams through AI that fits the way they already operate. DevelMo helps startups, enterprises and growing teams build intelligent products, automate operations and turn live data into decisions, through AI, computer vision, cloud and custom software.

DevelMo began with a simple conviction: most companies do not need more technology, they need the right technology applied well. Too many AI projects stall because they are built in isolation from the business they are meant to serve. DevelMo starts from the problem rather than the tool.

**Philosophy**: Technology should fit your business, not the other way around. Most off-the-shelf software forces you to change how you work; DevelMo flips that model, building custom AI and software solutions around a client's needs, data, and goals — offering both ready-made products and fully tailored solutions, no one-size-fits-all.

### 3.1 CrowdIQ (flagship product) [Verified today]

Tagline: "See Beyond the Crowd." Turns existing cameras into live business intelligence — visitor detection, tracking IDs, dwell time and heatmaps, in real time, at **90–95% person-detection accuracy**.

**Positioning**: the product page frames the problem as "Cameras record. They don't explain" — most CCTV systems capture footage but never turn it into insight, so teams end up guessing at footfall, dwell time and behaviour. CrowdIQ is pitched as a plug-and-play AI analytics layer for cameras a business already owns.

**What it does**: connects directly to local or networked cameras (RTSP, ONVIF, HTTP/HTTPS, webcams) and uses custom-trained AI models — not generic APIs — to deliver visitor detection with tracking IDs, age/gender estimation (including culturally-specific attire recognition), dwell-time and heatmap-based behaviour analysis, hourly traffic/demographic breakdowns, and full exportable/filterable CSV logs.

**Full current feature list**: camera connectivity (RTSP/ONVIF/HTTP/local/webcam, no new hardware); live inference on existing feeds; visitor detection at 90–95% accuracy; unique tracking IDs; age & gender estimation; cultural-attire recognition; dwell-time measurement; heatmaps & flow visualization; reports & exportable CSV logs; live analytics dashboard; historical/custom-timeframe comparison; flexible deployment (cloud, on-premise, or edge).

**How it works**: 1) Connect cameras. 2) Run detection (real-time, custom-trained model). 3) Analyze visitors (age, gender, dwell, tracking). 4) Generate insight (dashboards, timelines, reports). 5) Export & act (CSV export feeding decisions).

**Use cases** (six named on the current page, broader than the "retail" framing in the original crawl): retail stores (footfall/dwell/conversion by zone), shopping malls (tenant traffic, common-area flow), events (crowd density and engagement), public spaces (movement patterns, safety monitoring), hospitality (guest flow, service-area demand), smart buildings (occupancy, space utilization).

**Deployment & privacy**: Cloud (fastest to launch, scales with camera count, managed/updated by DevelMo); On-premise & edge (footage stays inside the client's network, processed close to the cameras); Privacy-aware by design (focuses on patterns/aggregates, secure architecture throughout — no facial-recognition/personal-ID claim made specifically for this product's default retail deployment).

**Pricing tiers** (figures unchanged from the original crawl; feature bullets have been reworded on the live page — current wording below):
- **Starter** — Free, 7-day trial. "Try it on a single feed": 1 camera source, live detection, core dashboard, visitor counts.
- **Business** — the featured middle tier, for SMEs & retail chains: **$299/month** (shown discounted from a $1,999 reference price). Multiple camera sources, age/gender & dwell analytics, heatmaps & visitor flow, CSV exports & historical comparison.
- **Enterprise** — Custom pricing, "fully customized": custom-trained models, on-prem or edge deployment, integrations & SLAs, dedicated support.

**FAQ on the live page** (paraphrased): what cameras are supported (anything with RTSP/ONVIF/HTTP, plus local/webcams — no hardware replacement needed); detection accuracy (90–95% typical, further tuned on Enterprise); how privacy is handled (cloud/on-prem/edge options so data stays where needed); whether it runs on-premise (yes, Enterprise supports on-prem/edge so video never leaves the network); setup time (most teams see live insight from a first camera the same day).

### 3.2 OmniRoad 2.0 [Verified today]

Tagline: "AI traffic intelligence for safer roads and smarter cities."

**What it does**: connects to existing cameras, detects and classifies vehicles in real time, and flags accidents the moment they happen — turning ordinary road footage into live situational awareness for traffic and safety teams. Recognises cars, trucks, buses and motorcycles, tracks road usage, identifies accidents and assesses severity, then pushes alerts to the people who need to act, without ripping out existing camera infrastructure.

**Key capabilities**: real-time object detection/tracking of vehicles by type; vehicle classification for detailed road-usage analysis; real-time accident detection with severity assessment; immediate alerts including instant sound notifications to relevant authorities; multi-protocol camera integration (works with virtually any existing setup); detailed road-usage analytics (flow, congestion, movement trends); actionable outputs designed to feed traffic management and emergency response, not just dashboards; scales from single junctions to city-wide camera networks.

**Who it's for**: traffic management authorities, road safety teams, smart-city programmes, and transport-hub operators (airports, bus stations, train stations).

**How it works**: 1) Connect cameras (multi-protocol, no hardware replacement). 2) Detect and classify in real time. 3) Spot accidents and assess severity. 4) Alert and act (including sound notifications). 5) Analyse and improve (road-usage data feeds ongoing safety/congestion work).

**FAQ on the live page** (paraphrased): no new cameras are required in most cases; it detects/classifies vehicles by type and flags accidents with severity; on accident detection it raises immediate alerts, including audible ones, to relevant authorities and emergency services.

No public pricing is shown for OmniRoad 2.0, confirmed on today's check (consistent with the original crawl).

### 3.3 PadelIQ [Verified today]

Tagline: "Structured matches, real rankings, and AI that reads the game."

**What it is**: the platform behind the **Riyadh Padel Federation (RPF)**, pairing a competitive team-based league with computer-vision analytics that turn match footage into on-court insight. Began as a structured community league (teams, results, transparent rankings, seasonal tournaments including the RPF Grand Slam) and has grown into an organised platform; the PadelIQ analytics layer reads movement, positioning, and rally patterns from match footage.

**Reported scale** (unchanged): 110+ active members, 50+ teams, 2+ competitive seasons, 540 points top-player season tally.

**Key capabilities**: form official partnered teams; record match results in seconds from the court; live leaderboards updating on every logged match; invite-based onboarding to keep rankings trustworthy; seasonal league play and tournaments year-round including the RPF Grand Slam; computer-vision analytics tracking movement/reaction time/positioning/rally patterns from footage; automated performance metrics; AI insights for clubs, coaches, and serious players; a transparent points engine feeding team/player rankings.

**Who it's for**: competitive padel players, partnered teams, clubs, and coaches wanting organised league play with honest rankings plus a performance data layer.

**How it works**: 1) Join the league via an invite code from an existing member/admin. 2) Register a team (pair with a partner). 3) Record and rank (submit results from the court; points engine updates instantly). 4) Analyse with PadelIQ (feed match footage in for automated movement/positioning/rally analysis).

**FAQ on the live page** (paraphrased): joining requires an invite code from an existing member or admin; it's a team-based league so a partner is required; rankings update automatically from every logged match result.
